# Data licences

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the source code only. It does **not** cover the data.

The data files under `app/public/` (the baked datasets, images and textures), and the
screenshots under `docs/images/` insofar as they show those data, keep the licence each
publisher gave them. Those licences are public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, ODbL,
non-commercial terms, or citation only.

If you reuse a data file, follow its licence and keep its attribution:

- [docs/DATA-SOURCES.md](docs/DATA-SOURCES.md) lists every source with its licence and the
  attribution it asks for. The share-alike, non-commercial and citation-only sources are
  listed together at the top.
- The per-topic notes in [docs/data-sources/](docs/data-sources/) give each full citation
  and how its file was made.
- The app shows the same list under **Layers → Data credits**.
