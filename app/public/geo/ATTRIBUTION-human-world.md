# Human-world layers: data sources and licences

Generated by `tools/pipeline/generate_cables.py`, `generate_landmarks.py`,
`generate_power_plants.py`, `generate_ship_density.py`, `generate_waters.py`
and `generate_air.py`. Details and verification: `docs/data-sources/human-world.md`.

| File | Content | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cables.json` | Undersea communication cables and landing points, as of 14 November 2022 | [TeleGeography, Submarine Cable Map](https://www.submarinecablemap.com/), GitHub repository at commit `91265d812a` | [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/) — **non-commercial**; this file is shared under the same licence |
| `air-routes.json` | Air routes of June 2014 | [OpenFlights](https://openflights.org/data.php) route database; airport positions from OurAirports | [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) — this file is shared under the same licence |
| `airports.json` | Large airports | [OurAirports](https://ourairports.com/data/); scale ranks from Natural Earth | Public domain |
| `waters.json`, `ports.json` | Rivers, lakes, sea ports | [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) | Public domain |
| `power-plants.json` | Power plants of 500 MW and more; all nuclear plants | Global Energy Observatory, Google, KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm, Enipedia, World Resources Institute (2021), [Global Power Plant Database v1.3.0](https://datasets.wri.org/dataset/globalpowerplantdatabase) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `ship-density.jpg` | Shipping traffic density, 2015–2021 | Cerdeiro, Komaromi, Liu & Saeed (2020), IMF Working Paper 20/57; [Global Shipping Traffic Density](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037580), World Bank Data Catalog | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `landmarks.json` | Mountains, deep places, launch sites, telescopes and detectors, accelerators, World Heritage Sites, wrecks | [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/); the depths of the Mponeng and TauTona mines from English Wikipedia | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); the two depths [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
